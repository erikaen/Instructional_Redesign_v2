## The ERTC chase record (2026-07-27)

Question (registry #4): the $2,913,362 "Employee Retention Tax Credits
income" line — received or receivable? Findings from the PDF:

- The line appears **exactly once** in the document: on the activities
  statement face, without-donor-restrictions column, 2025 ($0 in 2024).
- **No note covers it.** All 22 note headings checked; the policies note,
  Commitments and Contingencies — silent. (The only "retention" in the
  notes is the 401(k) plan.)
- **No ERTC receivable line** on the SFP, and — decisive — **no ERTC line
  in the CFS**: neither an adjustment nor a working-capital change names
  it. By the doc's core insight (the indirect CFS is the wedge inventory),
  an income line with no wedge line closed in cash the same year.
  Accounts receivable *fell* 300,616, fully accounted for by the CFS
  change (216,333) plus the allowance (84,283).

**Conclusion: Reasons → Cash, $2,913,362, received in FY2025 — tagged
INFERENCE.** The tag is the pedagogy: the statements never say it outright;
the student infers it from an *absence* — "no wedge line names this walk,
so the walk closed in cash." (Externally consistent: IRS ERTC backlog
payouts landed 2024–25; recognition on receipt with $0 prior-year is the
conservative treatment.)

## Engine deltas (`graph-lab.js` — append-only)

1. **Pile-to-pile transfer arrows** — an arrow type with both ends in the
   REASONS region (releases, appropriations); zero-sum styling distinct
   from give/receive exchange arrows.
2. **The partitioned Reasons region (R5)** — REASONS splits into
   With / Without Donor Restrictions sub-regions; nodes declare a pile;
   transfer arrows cross the partition line.
3. **The Value change Reason (R1)** — a Reason node, not a badge. Engine
   need: nothing structurally new (it gives/receives like any Reason), but
   its arrows should carry the re-pricing caption idiom ("no exchange —
   information re-priced the holding") for hover text.
4. **Drill-down explode** — a node that explodes into its sub-nodes and
   folds back (the 73-1 channel enclosure, generalized): used by 64-2
   (three → eighteen), 64-6 (net → gross), and the FE-row expense grain.
