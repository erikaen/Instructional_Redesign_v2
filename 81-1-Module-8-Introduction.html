Module 8 Introduction

The turn

You already built the machine

You built it three times: first as the workbook, then as the statement map, then as the graph. Each time, you used this course’s own words so you could see what the machine does before learning its labels.

Now you will learn the profession’s words for the same parts and moves. Those words are five hundred years old. They appear everywhere accounting appears.

Why the words matter

An analyst has to read the notation

Real artifacts use it: entries in accounting systems, auditors’ language, footnotes, and the titles on real statements. You need the words to read those artifacts closely and to reason backward from statements to the entries that produced them.

You will never keep books in this notation. That is not the job. Your job is to read what the notation says, connect it to the machine you already understand, and ask what the finished statements imply.

Nothing in this module is new; only the names are. This module is a translation.

What the module covers

The professional labels for work you already did

  • Debits and credits
  • Journal entries
  • T-accounts
  • Posting
  • The chart of accounts
